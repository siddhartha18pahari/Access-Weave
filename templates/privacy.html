Privacy & safety

Privacy by design. The system minimizes data collection at every stage.

Defaults that protect you

  • No diagnosis is ever required.
  • Uploaded images are processed in memory and not stored.
  • Location is off by default.
  • Your Access Passport holds preferences, not medical records.
  • Nothing is sent or shared without your confirmation.

Safety boundaries

  • Medical actions are never automatic.
  • Dosages and warnings are shown verbatim and never altered.
  • Uncertain, machine-read content is clearly marked for you to verify.
  • Content from files/webpages is treated as data, guarding against injection.

Your controls

You can delete any task and its source at any time. You can also erase everything now.

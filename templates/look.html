Look around

Point your camera at what's in front of you and I'll say what I see — with rough position (left, ahead, right). Everything runs on your device: no video or photo is ever uploaded.

Press Start, then allow camera access.

Tips

  • Move the camera slowly — I re-describe the scene as it changes.
  • Ask any time with “What do you see?” (or say it out loud with the voice button).
  • I recognise everyday objects (people, furniture, cups, phones, vehicles, and more) — not fine detail or text. For text on a label, use Start a task and the camera reader.

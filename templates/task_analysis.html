Accessibility contract check

Every compiled experience is validated against deterministic accessibility rules before you see it.

  • Every card has an accessible name and a text equivalent for speech.
  • Consequential actions (submit, pay, share) require explicit confirmation.
  • Low-confidence or safety-sensitive content is marked for you to verify.
  • Multi-step tasks include progress and a recovery path.
  • Safety instructions (like dosages) are shown verbatim and never altered.

Sound Watch

For when you can't hear what's around you: I watch for loud sounds — a doorbell, an alarm, a knock — and alert you visually and with vibration. Everything runs on your device; no audio is recorded, stored, or uploaded.

Press Start, then allow microphone access.

Higher = react to quieter sounds.

Recent sounds

    Only the time and a rough description are kept on this page — never the audio itself. This list clears when you leave.

    Good to know

    • The alert is a single slow colour pulse — never flashing or strobing.
    • On phones, you'll also feel a vibration.
    • I describe the sound's pattern (short, repeating, continuous) — I can't identify exactly what made it.
    • It keeps watching when you switch tabs. Detection runs on the browser's audio thread, which isn't paused for background tabs. Turn on “Alert me in other tabs” so a system notification reaches you there.
    • Keep this tab open — closing it stops the watch.
